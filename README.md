newspaper front page: 40 stories across 6 continents, led by the GTA VI
launch (a record $1.84bn day one), the Anthropic IPO debut ($97.17, up 17%),
and the first-ever Xi–Trump–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen.

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
and validates it against the gate: at most 1 MiB, exactly 1024×1024 pixels.
